Data deletion

How to delete data related to Varolus Studio apps.

This page explains how to delete data related to the mobile apps Varolus Studio publishes on Google Play. Our apps have no user accounts, and we do not store any of your data on our servers, so there is no account to close.

Delete the data an app keeps on your phone

Progress, settings and any photos you added are stored only on your device. To delete them, do either of the following:

  • Uninstall the app. Android removes all of its data.
  • Keep the app but clear its data: open Android Settings, tap Apps, choose the app, tap Storage, then tap Clear data.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Purchases are not affected; Google Play restores them when you reinstall.

Data held by Google for ads and purchases

Where an app shows optional ads or offers purchases, Google processes related data under its own policies. You can:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, under Privacy, then Ads;
  • change your ad consent choice from the app's privacy options in Settings;
  • manage or delete Google account data, including purchase history, at myaccount.google.com.

Emails you sent us

If you contacted us by email and want that correspondence deleted, email varolusstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
