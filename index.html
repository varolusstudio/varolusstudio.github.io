How an engagement runs

Short, verifiable steps. You see working results early and own everything at the end.

  1. Scope

    Agree the question to answer, the data available and the acceptance criteria before any code is written.

  2. Prototype

    A working model or pipeline within weeks, built against your real data rather than a demo set.

  3. Validate

    Check results against test data and document every assumption and limit, so the numbers can be trusted.

  4. Deploy and hand over

    Integrate with your tools, train your team, and hand over source code and documentation.
