App support

Help for the mobile apps Varolus Studio publishes on Google Play.

Reporting a bug? Email varolusstudio@gmail.com with the app name and version (shown in its Settings), your phone model and Android version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps. We reply within two business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in to Google Play with the account you used to buy it, then open the app. Purchases are restored automatically, and each app also has a restore option in its shop screen.

I reinstalled an app and my progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your phone; there are no accounts or cloud saves. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes it. Purchases are not lost, because Google Play restores them.

Do the apps work offline?

Yes. Core features work without a connection. Watching an optional ad and making a purchase need one.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open the app's Settings and choose the privacy options entry. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Follow Google's refund instructions, or email us and we will help.

How do I delete my data?

All app data lives on your device. Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings, then Apps, choose the app, then Storage, then Clear data. We hold none of your data on our side. See the privacy policy.
