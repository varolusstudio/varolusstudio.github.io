Support

Need help with Timber Slide: Picture Puzzle? Email varolusstudio@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Reporting a bug? Please include your phone model, Android version, the app version (shown in Settings), and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Make sure you are signed in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy Premium, then open the App. Purchases are restored automatically. You can also tap Restore purchase in the Shop screen.

I reinstalled the game and my progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your phone; there are no accounts or cloud saves. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes your progress. Premium purchases are not lost because Google Play restores them.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. All puzzles you have unlocked can be played without an internet connection. Watching a rewarded ad and buying Premium need a connection.

Are my own photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos you turn into puzzles stay in the App's private storage on your phone. You can delete them from the in-app gallery. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the App. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can request a refund through Google Play following Google's refund instructions, or email us and we will help.

How do I delete my data?

All game data lives on your device. Uninstall the App, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Timber Slide → Storage → Clear data. We do not hold any of your data on our side.
