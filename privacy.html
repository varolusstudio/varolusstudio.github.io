Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Varolus Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app Timber Slide: Picture Puzzle (the "App") and on this website. The short version: we do not run accounts, servers or analytics, and we do not collect your personal data ourselves. The only data that leaves your device is processed by Google for optional rewarded ads and for purchases, as described below.

1. Who we are

Varolus Studio is an independent developer based in Pakistan. Contact: varolusstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following inside its private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

  • Game progress: stars, best scores, unlocked packs and grids, hint tokens and a snapshot of an unfinished puzzle.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, Zen mode and display options.
  • Your own photos (Premium feature): if you choose to make a puzzle from your own photo, you pick it with the Android Photo Picker. The App receives only the photo you selected, makes a resized copy in its private storage and uses it only to build your puzzle. Photos are never uploaded or shared. You can delete them at any time from the in-app gallery.

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently deletes all of this information. We cannot recover it because we never receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App can show rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example, to earn extra hints or a temporary pack trial). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (views, taps, completions) and diagnostic data such as crash information from the ad SDK.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Data is sent over encrypted connections. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with applicable privacy laws, the App shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the App. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings.

If you buy Premium, the App no longer offers or requests ads.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases, such as the one-time Premium upgrade, are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details, name or billing address. The App receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can unlock what you bought and restore it after a reinstall. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs added by us.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files beyond the single photo you pick.

7. Children

The App is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App in a way that caused personal information to be collected by an ad partner, please contact us and we will help you take the appropriate steps.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Game data on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. For data processed by Google for ads or purchases, you can exercise your rights through Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com, your device's ad settings, or the in-app Privacy options. Residents of the EEA, UK and other regions with data protection laws may also contact us with any question or complaint, and may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

9. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We use no cookies, analytics or tracking on it. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's features or the services it uses change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
