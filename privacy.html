Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026. Applies to the mobile apps Varolus Studio publishes on Google Play and to this website.

This policy explains how Varolus Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile apps we publish on Google Play (each an "App") and on this website. In short: our Apps have no accounts, no servers of ours and no analytics, and we do not collect your personal data ourselves. Where an App offers optional rewarded ads or in-app purchases, that data is processed by Google, as described below.

1. Who we are

Varolus Studio is an independent software development studio based in Pakistan. Contact: varolusstudio@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

Our Apps keep the following in their private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else:

  • Progress and records, such as scores, unlocked content and an unfinished session you can resume.
  • Settings, such as sound, vibration and display options.
  • Photos you choose to use, where an App lets you use your own pictures. You select them with the Android Photo Picker, so the App receives only the photo you pick. It keeps a resized copy in private storage, never uploads or shares it, and lets you delete it at any time.

Uninstalling an App, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently deletes this information. We cannot recover it because we never receive it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some Apps offer rewarded video ads that play only when you choose to watch one, for example to earn a hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, app version and language,
  • ad interactions (views, taps, completions) and diagnostic data such as crash information from the ad SDK.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, to personalise them where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. Data is sent over encrypted connections. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with applicable privacy laws, an App shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from the App's privacy options in Settings, and you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings.

If you buy an App's ad-free or premium upgrade, that App no longer requests ads.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details, name or billing address. The App receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, so it can unlock what you bought and restore it after a reinstall. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in in our Apps.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs added by us.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files beyond a photo you pick yourself.

7. Children

Our Apps are designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's information was collected by an ad partner through one of our Apps, contact us and we will help you take the appropriate steps.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about App users, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. App data on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. For data processed by Google for ads or purchases, use Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com, your device's ad settings, or the App's privacy options. You may contact us with any question or complaint, and residents of the EEA, UK and similar jurisdictions may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

9. This website and enquiries

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us about a project or for support, we use your email address and message only to reply and to carry out any work you ask for, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

See our data deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

11. Changes to this policy

If our Apps or the services they use change, we will update this page and the date above.
